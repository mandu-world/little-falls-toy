# Little Falls

A tiny waterfall. Drops come over the mossy edge and fall into the pool.

- Tap somewhere to put a stone in the way.
- Tap a stone to take it away.
- The drops bounce off the stones. Watch where they go.
- Stones get dark and shiny when the water hits them. Out of the water, they dry slowly.
- When a drop hits the pool, sometimes tiny bits of water jump back up.

I made it after sniffing around the little falls. The real ones are wetter.
